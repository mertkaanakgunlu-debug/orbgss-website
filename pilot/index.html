Pilot

Kızıldere: the first OrbGSS application.

Kızıldere is a 36 × 36 km pilot area in Denizli, Türkiye, on a fixed 30 m grid (EPSG:32635). It is where the evidence-to-priority workflow has been applied for the first time, and where its evidence and its gaps can both be shown concretely.

01

AOI & project context

Every layer in this pilot is registered to the same 36 × 36 km frame, so what follows can be compared directly, layer to layer, on identical ground.

Source and AOI context only — not validation evidence.

AOI · EPSG:32635 · 30 m · 36 × 36 km

02

Terrain

NASADEM elevation is carried as context and display only. It gives the pilot area its physical shape; it is not a scored predictor and carries no universal geothermal-favourability direction.

Context and display only. Terrain is not a scored predictor and carries no universal geothermal-favourability direction.

Context · NASADEM elevation · metres

03

Evidence

One Landsat thermal anomaly layer (THM-01) and two Sentinel-2 spectral alteration proxies (ALT-01, ALT-02) form the accepted remote-sensing evidence core. They are candidate, unvalidated evidence — not mineral identification, and not proof of hydrothermal alteration.

Evidence layers only — candidate and unvalidated. THM-01 is thermal evidence, not geothermal probability, reserve or resource, discovery or drilling-success evidence. ALT-01 and ALT-02 are broad spectral alteration proxies, never mineral or kaolinite identification, and not proof of hydrothermal alteration.

Evidence · THM-01 / ALT-01 / ALT-02

04

Structure & geology

No public-safe fault or lithology layer is authorized for this pilot yet. That is shown as an explicit gap rather than filled in, and the gap does not change the priority baseline: structure and geology are optional support and score-invariant.

Structure and geology are optional support and score-invariant. Their absence does not change the remote-sensing priority baseline.

Optional support · Not published

Data gap

No fault or lithology layer is published for this area. Nothing is drawn here on purpose: an invented structural line would look like evidence and would not be.

Structural context — optional support / data gap

05

Priority

The thermal and alteration evidence is integrated into mvp_remote_sensing_priority_v1: one within-AOI ranking surface, 0 to 100. It is AOI-relative experimental screening, not a probability, a reserve or resource estimate, or a score calibrated across areas.

AOI-relative experimental screening only. Not probability, reserve or resource estimation, discovery likelihood, drilling-success likelihood, Full Prospectivity, or a score calibrated across areas.

Score · mvp_remote_sensing_priority_v1

First application

First-application proof, not field validation, discovery, reserve or resource, drilling-target or drilling-success proof.
