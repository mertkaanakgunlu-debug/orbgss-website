## 1. Direction decision

Direction C is rejected.

Do not continue the editorial-science-led / publication-like homepage direction.

The refinement target is a deliberate hybrid:

- **Direction A** supplies the immersive spatial composition and visual scale.
- **Direction B** supplies the interaction model, hierarchy and commercial/product clarity.

This is not a request to average the two concepts. Each section has a specific owner pattern below.

## 2. Hero

The accepted WEB-005A hero remains immutable.

Design prototypes may use a placeholder representation only when the design tool cannot access the exact production media. The implementation authority remains the real accepted hero, not the prototype placeholder.

## 3. Act 02 — use Direction A

Use A's image-led spatial treatment as the basis:

- one large natural-colour EO image;
- copy integrated into the image/protected shade;
- strong sense of place and geographic scale;
- avoid detached text-panel + image-panel treatment;
- preserve truthful context/metadata and AOI framing.

The exact imagery in the concept is not authoritative; production must use approved/provenance-safe Kızıldere context media.

## 4. Act 03 — use Direction B

Use B's single-frame evidence interaction as the basis:

- one dominant shared geographic frame;
- accessible selector/list/tabs for Terrain, THM-01 and ALT-01;
- selected state visibly changes beyond colour alone;
- same-ground/same-grid relationship is explicit;
- subtle crossfade only;
- no three-equal-card gallery.

The interaction must retain a no-JS fallback and reduced-motion behavior.

## 5. Act 04 — refine beyond the current A/B prototypes

Neither current concept is accepted as final for the result.

Keep B's product clarity and hierarchy, but increase A-level visual payoff:

- the Priority result must be the dominant post-evidence object;
- it should feel like the conclusion of the spatial story, not a report figure;
- preserve governed palette/legend/caveats;
- avoid a small contained card;
- avoid turning the result into a dashboard;
- explore one large map-led composition with concise definition/proof structure around it.

This section requires a dedicated refinement before CTO acceptance.

## 6. Domains — use Direction A scale with B clarity

Use large, image-led cards:
- Geothermal — active / first application
- Mining — in development
- Marine — in development

Prefer A's larger visual presence and stronger imagery.

Preserve B's clear status labelling and commercial scanability.

The concept-board illustrative domain imagery is not production authority. Shipping assets require rights/provenance confirmation and truthful captioning.

## 7. Differentiators / proof — use Direction B

Use B's compact qualitative proof structure.

Keep it:
- icon-light or icon-free;
- restrained;
- enterprise-legible;
- qualitative only until an owning domain publishes verifiable metrics.

Do not invent accuracy, success-rate, customer, scale or ROI figures.

## 8. Motion and atmosphere

Target "wow" through scale, imagery, hierarchy and restrained interaction, not decorative effects.

Allowed:
- subtle evidence crossfade;
- minimal hover/selection transitions;
- low-noise ambient motion in trust/reference areas if it remains subordinate.

Do not add a second heavy interactive globe or hero-like spectacle.

## 9. Mobile

The hybrid must be designed explicitly for mobile rather than merely collapsed from desktop.

Act 02:
- preserve image-first immersion;
- keep copy readable without covering critical geography.

Act 03:
- selector above the shared frame;
- ensure the frame remains visible without excessive scroll distance.

Act 04:
- result remains visually dominant;
- caveats/definition content stacks after the map rather than pushing it far below the fold.

Domain cards:
- large image-led cards stack vertically with clear status.
