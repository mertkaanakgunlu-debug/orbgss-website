About

OrbGSS — Orbital Geo-Spatial Solutions

OrbGSS is a geospatial-intelligence platform. It exists to make exploration and land decisions evidence-based, traceable and easier to prioritize.

Too much ground, too little time.

Exploration and land decisions begin with more ground than anyone can walk. The hard question comes before any fieldwork: where to look first.

Evidence that narrows the search.

OrbGSS publishes the evidence layers, their limits and their gaps beside every ranking, so a team can judge the result instead of trusting it.

Remote sensing can order the ground. Only the field can confirm it.

Principles

How OrbGSS publishes

  1. Evidence-backed screening

    Priorities come from mapped layers, not an unexplained model output.

    The three layers →

  2. Traceable provenance

    Every published visual maps back to a source and a recorded export.

    Data sources →

  3. Explicit data gaps

    A missing layer is published as missing, never invented.

    Structure and geology →

  4. AOI-first analysis

    One declared area, projection and grid before any interpretation.

    The pilot area →

  5. Remote narrowing

    Large areas are ordered from orbit before field time is committed.

  6. Decision support

    Helps decide where to investigate; field investigation remains necessary.

No accuracy, performance, deployment, customer or scale figures are published for this baseline.
