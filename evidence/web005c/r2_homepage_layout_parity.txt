WEB-005C R2 / MER-149 - whole-homepage layout parity, before vs after the card wiring

Why this instrument and not a pixel diff: a full-page screenshot of this page is NOT
deterministic. Two captures of the SAME tree differ by ~1 % of pixels at up to 8/255, because
lazy image decode order shifts the ordered dither in the page's gradients. A pixel diff
therefore cannot prove anything at that precision. This snapshot is deterministic: for every
element on the page it records the layout box plus the computed styles that carry the design
(font, colour, background, border, padding, margin, opacity, transform, display, position,
z-index, text-decoration, letter-spacing, line-height, text-transform, object-fit/position,
grid-template-columns, gap).

The three <a class="domain-link"> wrappers are the change itself, so they are excluded; every
OTHER element on the page is compared.
