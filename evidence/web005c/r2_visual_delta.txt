CONCLUSION
  The <a> wrapper that makes each card a link is pixel-free: A/B 2 is 0 differing pixels at
  every viewport. The entire measured delta comes from the transparent overlay that extends
  the click target across the whole card: being a positioned descendant, it makes Chrome
  rasterise .domain-shade's gradient into a different backing store, and the ordered dither
  lands one step differently. Bounded at 7/255 on one channel, with ZERO pixels at or above
  16/255 anywhere. No geometry, type, colour token, spacing, imagery, status or hierarchy
  value changes - proved separately and deterministically by the 23-width geometry sweep and
  the whole-page layout parity snapshot.

  The alternative with a literally zero-pixel delta is to drop the overlay and let only the
  copy block navigate. That was not taken: R2 section 2 asks for the CARD to be the
  navigation surface, and a sub-perceptual dither in a dark gradient is not a design change.
