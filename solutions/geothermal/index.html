Kızıldere: the first published pilot.

Everything published for the pilot, in one place: where it is, which layers it holds, what each layer is not, and what is still missing.

Where the pilot is

Kızıldere lies in the Büyük Menderes graben, Denizli, Türkiye. It is the one area OrbGSS has published end to end, so every layer and limit can be inspected.

One area, one grid

Every layer is registered to this square, on one grid and one projection.

Every layer, and what it is not

Two context layers orient the reader and three evidence layers carry the signal. Each states what it must not be taken to mean.

  1. Natural colour

    Landsat 8 OLI, 2025-05-05 · Context

    Earth-observation context only. This is a natural-colour photographic composite of the area, not analytical evidence, not a scored input, and not the acquisition date of any evidence layer.

  2. Elevation

    NASADEM · Context

    Context and display only. Terrain is not a scored predictor and carries no universal geothermal-favourability direction.

  3. Thermal anomaly

    Landsat thermal bands · Evidence

    Evidence layer only. Do not describe it as geothermal probability, reserve, discovery, or drilling-success evidence.

  4. Alteration signal

    Sentinel-2 · Evidence

    Broad Sentinel-2 spectral alteration proxy; not mineral identification and not proof of hydrothermal alteration.

  5. Second alteration signal (iron)

    Sentinel-2 · Evidence

    Accepted, not shown yet

    Broad Sentinel-2 spectral alteration proxy; not mineral identification and not proof of hydrothermal alteration.

One ranking, with its limits

The evidence layers are combined into one experimental score, 0 to 100, that ranks ground inside this area only. A higher value says look here first; it never says a resource is there.

AOI-relative experimental screening only. Not probability, reserve/resource estimation, discovery likelihood, drilling-success likelihood, Full Prospectivity, or a calibrated cross-AOI score.

Not available: structure and geology

No public-safe fault or lithology layer

No public-safe fault or lithology layer is authorized for this pilot. The gap is shown, not filled, and it does not change the priority: structure and geology are optional support and score-invariant.
